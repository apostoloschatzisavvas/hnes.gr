Become a memberWho we are

The Hellenic Nuclear Energy Society (HNES) is a non-profit scientific society dedicated to the advancement of nuclear energy in Greece. We aim to serve as a source of independent scientific knowledge for the Greek public while functioning as a global networking hub for nuclear energy professionals and students within Greece and the Diaspora.

About HNES

Overview

HNES was founded in 2026 by more than 20 leading Greek nuclear professionals worldwide, to provide a structured scientific framework for the advancement of nuclear technology in Greece. The society serves as the central networking hub for the Hellenic nuclear diaspora, connecting experts in academia, industry, law, and maritime.

Our Mission

To facilitate a globally connected community of nuclear professionals that cultivates scientific excellence and public literacy, enabling the adoption of nuclear energy for Greece's sustainable development and energy security.

Our Vision

To achieve a resilient energy future for Greece through the strategic adoption of nuclear energy, underpinned by a public with access to objective scientific information and a globally connected community of Hellenic nuclear experts.
